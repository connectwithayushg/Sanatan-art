## Daily garland variations

The master statue's face, hands, posture, traditional colours, trishul, serpent, clothing and transparent background are held fixed. Only the garland changes:

- Day 1: Add a very fine loose strand of WHITE jasmine flowers below the rudraksha necklaces.
- Day 3: Add one restrained short ORANGE marigold garland around the neck, hanging low below the rudraksha necklaces.
- Day 4: Add a small WHITE jasmine-and-YELLOW marigold garland around the neck, hanging below the rudraksha necklaces.
- Day 5: Add a restrained single YELLOW marigold garland around the neck, a little fuller than other days but not covering hands.
- Day 6: Add a thin WHITE jasmine garland with a few small RED rose accents around the neck, below the rudraksha necklaces.
- Day 7: Add a delicate loose ORANGE-and-WHITE flower garland below the rudraksha necklaces.

Day 2 uses the unadorned master. These are devotional app illustrations derived from the selected mockup; they do not depict a named temple or assert a weekday ritual rule.
